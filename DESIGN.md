# vergex design

How the vergex web interface looks. It builds on the global design guide and repeats nothing from it: the guide has the principles, behaviour and workflow, this file has the identity and the values. Every value here is the one the tokens in `web/src/styles` use; the two change together.

## 1. Identity

Ink on warm paper: a trading ledger, not a terminal.

vergex is near-monochrome. A warm off-white canvas carries white panels separated by hairlines, and one warm near-black ink does everything an accent would: text, the primary button, the active tab, the focus ring, the price line. Nothing in the chrome is blue. The only saturated colours are the ones the market makes, green and red for long and short, up and down, gain and loss, so the eye goes to the numbers that matter and nothing else competes with them. Buttons are pills, fields and badges are 6px, and the panels they sit on are 16px; numbers are set in a monospace face with tabular figures. Type is Inter at three weights, and letter-spacing tightens only on the few large readouts. The dark theme is the same ledger at night: warm near-black, never blue-black. The result is dense and calm, closer to a well-set financial document than to an exchange dashboard.

Signature choices:

1. **Ink is the accent.** One near-black does the work a brand colour usually does, so no screen has a colour that means nothing.
2. **The market is the only colour.** Green and red appear only with the meaning the market gives them, and never as decoration or as a neutral state.
3. **Warm paper in both themes.** Canvas, surface and ink all carry a warm cast; a cool grey or a blue-black would read as a different product.
4. **Hairlines, not shadows.** Panels, cards, tables and inputs are separated by 1px lines and tint; only layers that float cast a shadow.
5. **Pills on panels.** Round buttons and 6px fields on 16px panels, with mono tabular numbers, are what make a vergex screen recognisable at a glance.

Reference products: Duna (warm monochrome fintech with a dark ink action and hairline elevation), Steep (analytics set like an editorial page, pill controls, nothing chromatic but one accent), Linear (dense working screens, near-black on off-white, a quiet shell).

Accent policy: ink is the accent. It fills the primary button, underlines the active tab, rings the focused control, colours links and the chart's price line, and tints selected rows. No other colour may take one of these roles, and ink is never used as a background for a whole region.

Domain colours: success and danger carry only the market's meaning and its direct consequences, listed above. A state that is neither, such as stopped, idle, not bound or unavailable, is muted text. Venues and models are named, not coloured: their icons are monochrome ink. Text selection is ink at 15%.

## 3. Type

- **Inter** for everything, at 400 for body text, 500 for labels, buttons, navigation and table headers, 600 for panel titles, dialog titles and the primary button. Weight 700 appears only in the wordmark.
- **IBM Plex Mono** for prices, balances, quantities, percentages, timestamps, identifiers and code, always with tabular figures. An Inter column of numbers also sets `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`.
- Scale: 11 (meta, table headers), 12 (secondary text, badges), 13 (table cells, dense rows, form text), 14 (body, buttons, inputs, panel titles), 16 (dialog titles), 20 (detail page title, a card's main readout, the pages in the phone's menu), 24 (a page's key figure), 28 (the one hero figure on a page). Nothing in between.
- Line height 1.5 for text, 1.25 for titles, 1.2 for readouts. Letter-spacing 0 up to 16px, -0.01em at 20px, -0.02em at 24 and 28px; the wordmark alone sits at -0.055em.

## 4. Shape and density

- Base unit 4px. Gaps between panels 16px; panel padding 16px; the page gutter is `--page-gutter` (28px, 24px under 1450px, 16px under 700px) and the top bar shares it.
- Control heights: 32 (compact: segmented items, buttons inside table rows and toolbars), 36 (default: inputs, selects, buttons, setting rows), 40 (large: the primary button of a dialog or sign-in form). A row of controls shares one height.
- Radius ladder, and nothing outside it: pill (`9999px`) for buttons, switches, avatars and segmented tracks and items; 6px for what holds a value (inputs, textareas, anything that picks from a list), for badges and tags, for rows in a list or menu and for blocks nested in a panel or dialog; 16px for panels, cards, popovers, menus, toasts, chart frames, dialogs and sheets; 4px for skeleton blocks and inline code.
- Density is dense: table rows 40px, panel headers 48px, setting rows 36px controls with 12px vertical padding, a 160px label column in settings forms.

## 5. Elevation and motion

| Level | Elements | Treatment |
| :---- | :------- | :-------- |
| Flat | Panels, cards, tables, inputs, the top bar | 1px `--line`, no shadow |
| Float | Popovers, menus, selects, tooltips | 1px `--line` and `--shadow-float`: `0 4px 16px rgba(26, 24, 22, 0.08)` light, `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(236, 232, 225, 0.08), 0 4px 16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)` dark |
| Overlay | Dialogs, toasts | 1px `--line` and `--shadow-overlay`: `0 16px 48px rgba(26, 24, 22, 0.14)` light, `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(236, 232, 225, 0.08), 0 16px 48px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.55)` dark |

Hover on a flat element changes its background to `--surface-muted` or its border to `--line-strong`; it never adds a shadow. Focus is a 3px ring of ink at 25% on buttons and switches. What holds a value is quieter, since it is where the eye already is: its border goes to ink at 40%, with a 1px ring of ink at 10%.

Motion uses the global defaults as tokens in `tokens.css`: `--dur-fade` 120ms, `--dur-state` 200ms, `--dur-panel` 320ms, `--ease-move` `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`, `--ease-fade` `ease-out`. The Dashboard stage is the product's one live view: an event travels its route within 600ms, groups of a burst follow each other 240ms apart, a lit member stays lit 700ms and a burst's summary stays 1.5s. Nothing on it moves without an event, but for the view following a drag, at once and within limits that keep everything in frame. The camera is fitted once for the stage's size and only backs away as the view tilts further down, as one that refits while turning makes the picture jump. Zoom takes Ctrl or Command, or a pinch, as on the charts, and a button in the stage's corner or a double click goes back to where it started.

## 6. Components

Deltas from the global guide, with the component in `web/src/components`.

- **Button** (`ui/button.tsx`): pill at every size except `select`; sizes `sm` 32px, `md` 36px, `lg` 40px, `icon` 36px circle; text 14/500 at every variant. Variants are flat fills, never a white box with an outline, which reads as a stock component: `primary` ink with `--on-ink` text, `--ink-hover` on hover; `secondary` `--ink-tint` with ink text, the tint of what is selected elsewhere, ink at 12% on hover; `danger` and `success` their soft background with their colour as text, the colour at 20% on hover; `destructive` danger with `--on-ink` text; `ghost` muted text, ink on `--surface-muted` on hover; `link` ink text, underlined on hover, no box; `segment` and `option` show the pressed choice as `--ink-tint` with ink text and, for `option`, an ink border, as a choice is not an action; `select` is the input look at 14/400, with its border at ink 40% while its list is open. In Settings a button carries a 16px icon, 6px from its text at `sm`: what it does comes first, as with a pencil for edit, and where it goes comes last, as with an arrow or an external link. On a phone a list row's buttons may be the icon alone.
- **Panel** (`common/panel.tsx`): surface, `--line`, 16px; header 48px with a 14/600 title and 12 muted meta; actions right. `status` is what comes and goes on that row, one line that opens in full in a popover, so nothing below it moves. `PanelState` centres a 14 muted line with a 20px faint icon.
- **Dialog** (`common/dialog.tsx`): 16px, `--shadow-overlay`, title 16/600, body text 13 to 14. `dialogInputClass` is the one input look: 36px, 6px, surface, `--line`, `--line-strong` on hover, the quiet focus of section 5, `--surface-muted` when disabled.
- **Settings windows** (`SettingList` in `common/setting-row.tsx`): each list and form scrolls between a head pinned at the top and a 57px bar pinned at the bottom, in the same place on all of them, so moving between windows moves no button. The bar holds what can be done, on the right, and one line about it on the left. A window with nothing to do has no bar.
- **Confirmation** (`common/confirm-dialog.tsx`, asked through `confirmToast`): text only, 400px with 24px padding: a title in 16/600, what it does in 14 ink soft, and the buttons on the right 20px below, sharing the width on a phone. No icon or hairline: the title names the action, the confirm button repeats it so the answer says what it does, and a destructive one is told by its danger-filled button alone. Cancel takes the focus, and it has no close button, as Cancel is that.
- **AppSelect** (`ui/select.tsx`): the trigger is the `select` button; the popover is 16px with `--shadow-float`, items 32px at 6px, highlighted on `--surface-muted`. A picker with a popover of its own, such as `MarketPicker`, has the same trigger and 6px rows.
- **Model name** (`settings/model-field.tsx`): the one list without the `select` trigger, because a model's name can be anything: a text field holding the one name in use, with a chevron at its end opening what the provider offers at the field's width. It has no search and keeps nothing typed; a pick replaces the text, and typing closes the list.
- **Popover** (`ui/popover.tsx`): 16px, `--line`, `--shadow-float`.
- **Switch** (`ui/switch.tsx`): track `--line-strong` off and ink on; thumb surface.
- **Segmented** (`common/segmented.tsx`): a pill track with a `--line` border and 32px pill items; the pressed item is `--ink-tint` with ink text at 500.
- **SectionTabs** and page tabs: 14/500 muted; the active tab is ink with a 2px ink underline.
- **Several things each on or off** in one form, such as which alerts to send: a grid of `option` pills under one `SettingRow`, equal in width, two across on a phone and three above that, saved as they are pressed. A pressed pill means it is on. A row of switches would push the form's actions out of view.
- **Telegram setup** (`settings/telegram-settings.tsx`): `DialogSteps` centred above each step, as tall as `SettingBack`, and the rest on `SettingRow`'s grid. The step comes only from what is saved, so the flow runs one way and has no cancel: going back to the first step clears the token, after a danger confirmation. Creating a bot has what to do as a row in 13 muted, not a filled note, then the token and the model. Binding has the bot and its state, the token, the QR code and the code, with each row's action at its end, and the bar holds the wait with the code's time left and the one primary action. Once bound the steps go, and the first row falls where a list's first row does. The bot's state is the `SettingBadge` every connection has, and a row's figures or dates sit at its end in 12 muted.
- **SymbolsField** (`common/form-fields.tsx`): its input comes first and the chips it has added follow below; its `SettingRow` passes `top` so the name lines up with the input.
- **Top bar** (`common/header-bar.tsx`): surface with a `--line` bottom border; links 13/500 muted, the current page ink on `--ink-tint`. Under 640px it holds a smaller logo, the current page's name at 14/500 muted after a hairline, and one toggle of two 1.5px strokes that turn into a cross where they stand. The toggle unfolds a solid `--surface` sheet from the bar to the bottom of the screen, with no blur or scrim, and locks the page behind it: the pages as 56px rows at 20/400 muted, the current one ink on `--ink-tint` at 500, and the account's rows pinned at the bottom, so a phone has one menu rather than two.
- **Tables** (base styles in `styles/index.css`): headers 11/500 muted, rows 40px divided by `--line`, hovered rows ink at 3%; numeric cells mono tabular, right-aligned, unit named once in the header. A long value such as a provider's error keeps to one cut-off line, and pressing its row opens the whole of it below on `--surface-muted`, as with a Trader order's fills.
- **Badges** (`SettingBadge` and status badges): 6px, 11/500; success, danger and warning use their soft background and border; a neutral badge is `--surface-muted` with muted text.
- **Toast** (`.vergex-toast`): surface, `--line`, 16px, `--shadow-overlay`; the icon alone carries the type colour.
- **Charts** (`market-chart/market-chart-surface.ts`, `futures/futures-chart/`, `trader/equity-curve.tsx`): background surface, grid `--line`, axis text muted, crosshair faint; candles and volume success and danger; price and equity lines ink; plan levels entry ink, stop danger, target success, solid at 1px; what the account really holds dotted at 1px, in the market's colours; the agent's drawings muted and dashed; axis labels in mono. Forecast's indicator lines stay achromatic, told apart by shade and dash: VWAP ink, EMA 20 and RSI ink soft, EMA 50 muted and dashed, Bollinger faint. All of it reads the tokens and repaints on `THEME_EVENT`. On Forecast's chart (`market-chart/local-market-chart.ts`), which sits in a page that scrolls, an upright wheel scrolls the page: zoom takes Ctrl or Command, a pinch or the buttons in a pill that shows on hover, and the candle under the pointer reads in a line at the top left, in the candle's market colour. A line floating on a chart is `chartLineClass` (`market-chart/chart-legend.tsx`) on every chart: 8px from the top left, 11px mono on the surface at 85% with no blur, fields 8px apart and wrapping between them on a narrow chart.
- **MonoIcon** (`common/mono-icon.tsx`): a venue's or a model's mark in ink. The SVG only gives the shape, through a CSS mask.
- **Dashboard stage** (`dashboard/flow-stage.ts`, `flow-scene.tsx`, `flow-layout.ts`): the page's main surface, in WebGL on `--canvas`, following the theme. Every route runs left to right through four columns named along the front of the floor beside an arrow: data, engines, where they send things, what comes of it. Calls to a model run on an upper deck and orders and money on the ground, so the routes that would cross pass over each other, and a gate that reaches the upper deck stands taller. Each part of the system is a thin standing gate across its routes, never a platform, with its members as dots on its face that read from either side: ink when on, hollow when stopped, the market's colours for a position. A route is a curtain whose height follows its last day's events on a log scale, a hairline with none, with a line along its middle, fading in from where it leaves and carrying cone arrowheads that read from any side, so a still stage says which way. The view starts turned 0.62 rad towards where the flow begins, so the exchange stands nearest and the rest runs away from it, as head-on the gates would be edge-on. An event opens with a ring on the floor at its source and its member lit, then runs as a comet with a fading tail, glowing only on the dark theme. No text is drawn on the stage: names and figures are buttons in the page, standing directly on it with the canvas behind each stroke, moved to where the stage says, and a keyboard reaches the curtains through hidden buttons. It draws only when something changes, caps the pixel ratio at 2, holds still under reduced motion, and gives way to the phone's list where WebGL cannot run. It has no bar of its own: a gate or route gives its figures as a tooltip when hovered, and a stale notice stands in the stage's top corner only while the data has stopped.
- **Overview figures** (`dashboard/overview-figures.tsx`, `attention-list.tsx`): a `StatStrip` of equity, today's realized P/L, traders running and positions above the stage, with `AttentionList` always its last cell, on a phone its own row. It says "None" in 13 ink, or a 12px triangle and the count in warning that open a 360px popover of rows capped at 288px. There is no banner or filled alert: the warning reads as part of the system, and a problem appearing or clearing changes no region's size. Events and positions share the panel beside the stage, as choices of one `Segmented`.
- **Coin list** (`forecast/coin-list.tsx`): a 320px panel beside the page from `lg`, fixed in place and height: the page's tabs stay sticky above it, and it ends 24px above the screen's edge, level with the chart on the first screen and with the panels at the page's end, so scrolling moves nothing in it. Search and a filter stay pinned above its own scroll; under `lg` it opens as a popover from the coin trigger. Columns are pinned so values changing move nothing, and the last one keeps 16px from the edge, clear of the overlay scrollbar. The star is ink when on and faint when off, never gold.
- **Forecast reading** (`forecast/forecast-reading.tsx`): the chart is the page's main surface and fills the first screen. `ForecastCall` sits in the chart's head beside its interval and menu, with the price and the score at 20px; `ForecastFigures` is a panel under the chart, 12 muted names over 13 mono values in as many columns as fit.
- **Strategy picker** (`forecast/strategy-select.tsx`): beside the tabs on Signals, the page it decides. The `select` trigger at 208px with a muted label, opening a list as wide as the trigger with `AppSelect`'s rows: 32px, the name and a check on the one in use, the date in the row's title, search from seven strategies, and a way to where strategies are managed.
- **Split view** (`common/split-view.tsx`): a 320px list beside its detail, as on Forecast's Strategies and Backtests, with the same width and gap as the coin list on Signals so the detail's edge stays put between tabs, both filling the screen to the page's edge so the page does not scroll from `lg` up; below that, a select above the detail takes the list's place. The list has no panel: its entries are 6px rows on the page, the current one on `--ink-tint`, others `--surface-muted` on hover, and its add button is pinned beneath a hairline. An entry's actions are in a `…` menu that shows on hover or on the current entry, led by the switch that puts it to use or stops it, which its panel's header also has: primary to use, secondary to stop. The detail is a panel whose header and action bar stay put while its body scrolls, and whose table headings stick to the top of that scroll.
- **Name editing** (`NameInput`, `EditableTitle` in `forecast/name-editing.tsx`): a name edits where it stands, in its own font and place, and nothing moves. In a list the row itself turns into the field, one 1px `--line-strong` on `--surface` with no box inside it, and a check and a cross take the place of its `…`. As a panel's title the name's room is kept and a 28px field of at least 240px floats over it with the check and cross inside; what follows the name stays in place out of sight, so no word is left half covered, and a longer name grows over it. Neither takes the focus inputs take: the box exists only while focused, so it already marks the focus. A name refused turns the line `--danger`. The title's pencil shows when the title is hovered or reached by keyboard, and always on a touch screen.

Settings has the shape of Forecast's: the limits and preferences fill the left, with the preferences taking the height that is left, beside a 320px panel of entry calculators. Limits keep as they change and preferences save on leaving the field, so neither has a save bar.
- **Auto trading** (`pages/traders-page.tsx`, `trader/trader-panel.tsx`, `pages/traders-overview-page.tsx`): a `SplitView` of traders, each entry its name, a 6px dot for whether it runs, its kind and model, and its return in mono, beside a panel whose header and tabs stay while the tab scrolls. `TraderState` beside the name says how it stands, and takes a warning or danger tone and opens in a popover when trading is blocked or safe mode is on, so a problem moves nothing. Overview is one panel with a `StatStrip` and a table the column headers sort. A tab is flat, with no cards inside the panel: Performance runs from a `StatStrip` and a `FigureGrid` through the equity curve to one table of results by symbol, hold time and side; Positions, Orders and the rest use the figure tables, with a table's name in the first column of its head and its action in the last. Settings switches its parts with `Segmented`, with its save bar pinned, and what a running trader runs with is locked with the reason in that bar.
- **Skeleton** (`ui/skeleton.tsx`, `.skeleton`): `--line` at 4px, pulsing except under reduced motion, at about the size of the figure it stands for and on its line, so the row keeps its height. `PanelLoading` in `common/panel.tsx` is three of them where a region's rows will be, in the room its empty and failed states take.

## 7. Do and don't

Do:

- Give every action, selection and focus state the ink; one accent keeps the market's colours unchallenged.
- Use green and red only with their market meaning; a reader must be able to trust that a red cell is a loss, a short or a failure.
- Keep both themes warm: canvas, surface and ink share a cast, and the dark theme is a warm near-black.
- Separate flat elements with `--line` and `--surface-muted`; reserve shadows for popovers, dialogs and toasts.
- Set numbers in mono with tabular figures, right-aligned in columns, and large readouts at 20 to 28px in ink.
- End descriptive text without `.` or `。`, whether it is a hint, an empty state, an error or a bot's message: this interface is made of labels, and a full stop makes a line read as prose. A question keeps its mark, and several sentences in one string keep the marks between them.
- Stay on the radius ladder: pills for what acts, 6 for what holds a value and for badges, 16 for panels and dialogs. A value in a pill reads as a button, and the row stops saying which is which.
- Keep three weights and tighten letter-spacing only from 20px up; size and ink carry emphasis, not boldness.
- Show a figure being read as a `Skeleton` and a figure with nothing behind it (no trades, no positions, nothing realized) as 0 in its own format. This departs from the global guide's "Unavailable values show as —" because empty is the normal state of a new account, and a page of zeros reads calmer than a page of dashes. "—" stays only where 0 would state something false: a price an order does not have, a read that failed with nothing to fall back on (0 would say the money is gone), a time or a name.

Don't:

- Don't use blue anywhere: not in the chrome, the charts, the focus ring, links or selection. One stray blue reads as a leftover of another product.
- Don't colour a neutral state: stopped, idle, not bound and unavailable are muted text, and amber is only for something the user should act on.
- Don't give a venue or a model a brand colour; their icons are monochrome ink.
- Don't put a shadow on a panel, card or input, or a glow on focus beyond the ring.
- Don't put a status line in the page's flow. An error, a note or progress floats, as a toast or above what it concerns, or sits on a row that is there anyway, and opens in full from there; anything else moves what the user is reading each time it comes and goes.
- Don't explain a screen with a sentence. If a label, a state or the next step says it, the sentence goes; what stays is what changes how to read a figure, and what is about to happen with real money.
- Don't use gradients, blur, glass or textures on working screens; the sign-in family and the Dashboard's stage are the only places a scene is allowed. The stage is the showcase of the product, so its curtains may fade and its comets glow, because that is what shows direction and depth; it stays achromatic but for the market's colours, and it never moves without an event.
- Don't use weight 700 outside the wordmark, or make a number louder with bold instead of size.
- Don't invent a radius or a size: nothing at 8, 10, 12, 14 or 18px, no square buttons, no pill panels or badges.

## 8. Tokens in code

- `web/src/styles/tokens.css` holds every token and nothing else: the colour roles in section 2 in `:root` and `:root[data-theme='dark']`, the shadows in section 5, `--page-gutter`, the motion tokens, and the `@theme` block that turns them into classes. There is one name per role and no second set. `index.css` is only the entry that imports the fonts, Tailwind, `tokens.css`, `base.css` (element defaults and global utilities) and `shell.css` (top bar, page frame and tabs, toasts, the sign-in family); a page's own CSS sits beside the page.
- Colours reach Tailwind through `@theme inline` as `--color-vergex-<role>: var(--<role>)`, so components write `bg-vergex-surface`, `text-vergex-muted`, `border-vergex-line`, `text-vergex-success`, `bg-vergex-ink-tint`, and an opacity such as `ring-vergex-ink/25` mixes the current ink. Tailwind's own palette is switched off; `white` and `black` remain for text on a status fill and for overlays on a photo. CSS files use `var(--<role>)`.
- Shadows are written as `shadow-(--shadow-float)` and `shadow-(--shadow-overlay)` in components and `var(--shadow-float)` in CSS. Tailwind's own shadows are switched off, so `shadow-sm`, `shadow-md` and `shadow-lg` do not exist.
- The radius ladder is the only one Tailwind generates: `rounded` 4px, `rounded-md` 6px, `rounded-2xl` 16px, `rounded-full` pill. `rounded-sm`, `rounded-lg`, `rounded-xl` and `rounded-3xl` do not exist.
- Weights are `font-normal`, `font-medium` and `font-semibold`; sizes are the scale in section 3 as `text-[11px]`, `text-xs`, `text-[13px]`, `text-sm`, `text-base`, `text-xl`, `text-2xl` and `text-[28px]`.
- To add a token: add its light and dark values to both blocks in `tokens.css`, add it to `@theme inline` when components need it as a class, add its row to section 2, in one commit.
- Anything coloured from code reads a token with `getComputedStyle(document.documentElement).getPropertyValue('--role')` and repaints on `THEME_EVENT` from `contexts/theme-context.tsx`.

## 9. Showcase

The sign-in family (login, setup, reset password, reset user) shares one scene on the canvas, `AuthSurface` in `components/auth/auth-surface.tsx`: the wordmark, a 48px title in Inter 500 at -0.03em, narrowing to 32px on a phone, and a 400px form of 40px controls and one ink primary button. Behind them, a grid of `--line` at 80px fades out before the page's edges, like a ledger's ruled lines. No blurred shapes, gradient fills or colour; the page differs from a working screen only in its title size, its grid and its centred, unhurried spacing.
